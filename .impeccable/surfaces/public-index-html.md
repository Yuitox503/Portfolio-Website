## Direction contract

THESIS: Isotype wall chart. Facts about Ferdinand are drawn as counted units (figures, sheets, crates, blocks, ghost units for the unfilled remainder), and the page is a wall of flat enamel colour fields. Refuses the dark-neon AI-portfolio default (aurora, glow, grid, ticker, crosshair, loader, scramble).
