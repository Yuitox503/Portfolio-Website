# Surface brief: public/index.html (portfolio)

Mode: Experience (portfolio; the work leads). Visitor: a recruiter skimming under a minute, plus technical peers who inspect craft. Action: download the CV or email. Content, assets, downloads and links are frozen; design-only. Must keep the certificate/awards format (award cards, cert chips, masonry gallery with staggered reveal, lightbox), the Tracker nav link, obfuscated email, transcript menu.

Revision 2 (user feedback): the first build read as too many bold colours and too blocky; the old site's calmer colour was preferred; project galleries should flow like the original moving image strips. Palette now comes from Wada Sanzo, "A Dictionary of Color Combinations", combination no. 279.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A calm, tonal portfolio drawn as soft stacked sheets; the Isotype chart idea survives only in the stats (counted units with ghost remainders). Refuses loud multi-colour fields, hard blocks, and the dark-neon AI-portfolio default.

OWN-WORLD: Wada 279 only: Dark Tyrian Blue #12354e (with a darker #0d2739 and lighter #194560 for depth), Vinaceous Cinnamon #eea78c as the single accent, Ecru #c2ae93 for quiet lines and secondary fills, Raw Sienna #bb7125 for small marks, and a light paper tint of ecru #f3ece2. Shippori Mincho display, Zen Kaku Gothic New text. Pill buttons and tags, 24-32px radii, 40px rounded sheet tops, floating pill nav, wavy underline accent, flat colour, no shadows.

STORY: A visitor sees who he is and that he is open to work, reads the numbers as drawn units, scrolls through sheets of education, experience on a drawing rail and flowing project screenshots, filters work by skill, and leaves through the CV or email.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Deep blue field, 1440x900. Floating pill nav. Left: status pill, the name in large Mincho (second line in cinnamon), subtitle, contact line, bio, three pill CTAs (cinnamon primary). Right: the cut-out portrait on a lifted-blue disc with a cinnamon dot and a dashed ghost circle; slow drifting circles and a pointer-reactive halftone field behind. The next sheet curves up from the bottom edge.

FORM: Soft stacked sheets in a single Wada combination, position unchanged from the first build (isotype wall chart, seed:2761ef6152d456e1), restyled in response to the user's feedback; this is a revision of the same surface, not a new roll. Motion grammar: gentle reveals that remove themselves, compositor-only marquee with pause, nothing heavy.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
